# Code of conduct

## Commitment
WhisprLink is intended to be a constructive, respectful, and safety-conscious open-source project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to behave professionally and treat others with respect.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good faith in technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback about work, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal data, private messages, or credentials.
- Keep discussions relevant and inclusive.
- Report abuse and security concerns through the appropriate private channel.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination, or targeted abuse.
- Doxxing, stalking, or sharing private information without consent.
- Publishing credentials, private conversations, or exploit details that put users at risk.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith conduct.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit/remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or block contributors to protect the community.

## Reporting
For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through the GitHub profile. For security vulnerabilities, follow SECURITY.md. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as practical and handled confidentially where possible.
